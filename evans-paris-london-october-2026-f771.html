Autumn in the City of Light

By the middle of October, Paris has gone quiet in the best way. Two nights in Paris

Hotel Ami Orso

15th arrondissement · 3 star eco certified boutique · guest score 9.2 out of 10 from 223 reviews, and 8.8 elsewhere

Ami Orso sits in a residential pocket of the fifteenth that still behaves like a village. Bakeries, a couple of groceries, and casual neighborhood dining are within a block or two, and the hotel itself is built around a garden courtyard with a library and a lounge bar off it. The look is Nordic and pared back, all pale wood and quiet, which is a relief after a red eye. The building was renovated in 2021 and holds Green Key and FairMoove sustainability certifications.

The honest trade: this is not a walk out the door and see the Eiffel Tower address. The tower sits somewhere between 1.2 and 1.9 miles away depending on which source you read, so it is a long walk or a short Métro ride rather than a stroll. What you get instead is Vaugirard station one to five minutes from the front door, a genuinely quiet night's sleep, and a neighborhood where people actually live.

Border control happens in Paris, before you board

Because the UK sits outside the EU and the Schengen area, the border is juxtaposed. You clear French exit immigration and UK entry control back to back at Gare du Nord, before boarding. When you reach St Pancras there are no further checks. You step off the train and walk out of the station.

How much time to allow
  • Standard and Plus: arrive 75 to 90 minutes before departure. Automated ticket gates close strictly 30 minutes before the train leaves.
  • Premier: arrive 45 minutes before departure. Gates close strictly 15 minutes before the train leaves.
The sequence at Gare du Nord

The Eurostar departure terminal is on the first floor balcony level. You move through these checkpoints in order:

  • Scan your paper or mobile barcode at the automated ticket gates.
  • Complete the EU Entry Exit System kiosks. Non EU and British citizens finish digital passport verification, a photo capture, and fingerprint scans at the self service stations.
  • Pass French exit control, by e gate or manned booth, formally leaving the Schengen zone.
  • Present passports to UK Border Force, or use the UK biometric ePassport gates.
  • Clear security and baggage screening. There are no liquid volume limits and no weight limits, provided you can carry your own bags.
  • Wait in the secured international departure lounge. Boarding usually opens 20 minutes before departure, from platforms 3, 4, 5 or 6.
Documents to have ready before you reach the departure balcony. A valid biometric passport covering the length of your stay. An approved UK Electronic Travel Authorisation, digitally linked to that passport, required for non UK nationals traveling without a visa. And your Advance Passenger Information, submitted online through Eurostar before you arrive at the station. The EES registration is a one time step; future crossings move faster once you are in the system.

Royal Borough of Kensington and Chelsea · 4 star boutique in an 1876 Victorian townhouse
