Autumn in the City of Light

By the middle of October, Paris has gone quiet in the best way. The light turns low and gold by four in the afternoon, café terraces move under heat lamps, and the chestnut sellers set up near the Métro entrances. Two and a half hours west, London smells of wet stone and coffee, and the museums in Kensington are yours without the summer queue.

October 17, Saturday — Eurostar to London

A direct high-speed run through the Channel Tunnel, then three nights in Kensington.

Train · Reservation
Eurostar, Paris Gare du Nord → London St Pancras
$795 estimated for three

Departing Paris Gare du Nord at 2:02 PM, arriving London St Pancras in the mid-afternoon local time — a direct journey with no changes and no layovers, averaging 2 hours 28 minutes. Eurostar Standard class for all three: this figure is an estimate — published fares on this route range widely, and a live fare pull is needed before it's final on the client quote.

Border control: Because the UK sits outside the Schengen area, both French exit and UK entry checks happen at Paris Gare du Nord before boarding, including a photo and fingerprint check at the EU Entry/Exit System kiosks. There are no further checks on arrival in London — you simply step off the train.

Arrive early: Standard and Plus travelers should arrive 75–90 minutes before departure (gates close 30 minutes prior).

Documents to have ready: a biometric passport valid for the full stay, an approved UK Electronic Travel Authorization for non-UK nationals, and Advance Passenger Information submitted online before arriving at the station — the ETA is worth starting now, since it can take a few days to come through.

Still to confirm with the client: cathedral + palace, one stop or two

Two ways this can go, and it's worth a quick check with Francis or Rebecca before locking it in:

Option A — Westminster Abbey (one stop)

  • A 10-minute walk from Buckingham Palace, so it pairs naturally with the Guard ceremony in one morning
  • Technically a Royal Peculiar, not a cathedral, though most visitors treat it as one
  • Adult entry ~£29–31 (~$120–130 for three)
  • Closed to tourist visits on Sundays — Monday is fine

Option B — St Paul's Cathedral + Kensington Palace (two stops)

  • St Paul's is a genuine cathedral, but it's across town from the Palace — doesn't fit into the same morning as the Guard ceremony
  • Kensington Palace is a real interior walk-through, and it's right by the hotel
  • Kensington Palace adult entry ~£24.70 (~$100 for three); St Paul's pricing to be confirmed

This quote currently assumes Option A for the morning's flow — swap in Option B once the client weighs in, and the schedule and transfers above will need to adjust accordingly.
