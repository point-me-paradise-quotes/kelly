The hotel

A small boutique hotel in a quiet, village like corner of the 15th arrondissement, renovated in 2021 with a soft Nordic look layered over a Parisian townhouse feel. A garden courtyard, a small library, a lounge bar and a snack bar and deli give you somewhere to land at the end of a long day. It is the more relaxed and more affordable of your two Paris choices, and the trade is location: you are in a residential neighborhood rather than beside the monuments, with Vaugirard Metro station a short walk away to get you into the center.

The Eiffel Tower is roughly 1.2 to 1.9 miles from the hotel, the Louvre about 2 miles, and the Musée d'Orsay and Luxembourg Gardens about 2.1 miles. Cafés, casual restaurants and grocery stores are close by for coffee and a quick bite before heading out.

Included in the stay

  • Air conditioned, soundproofed rooms with blackout curtains and pillowtop beds

  • Garden, sun terrace, library and lounge bar

Not included

You leave from the middle of Paris and arrive in the middle of London, with no airport and no baggage carousel at the far end. Bring 2 pieces of luggage plus 1 small bag each; there are no liquid restrictions and no weight limit as long as you can carry your own bags. Free WiFi on board, UK and EU plug sockets at the seat, and a café car for drinks and snacks.

Allow extra time for border formalities
  • Passport checks for both leaving France and entering the UK happen at Gare du Nord before you board. Plan to be at the station 75 to 90 minutes before departure. Once you arrive at St Pancras you simply walk off the train.
  • Entry and travel document requirements for US citizens visiting the UK can change. Check the official UK government site (gov.uk) and the Eurostar site before you travel, and your advisor will go over what you need well before departure.
Cancellation and changes: exchange with no fee up to 1 hour before departure, paying any difference in fare. Refundable up to 7 days before departure for a fee of $40 (€25 / £25).

To Wembley, route 1: Underground and Chiltern RailwaysIn quote

Included in your total

Take the Underground from South Kensington to Edgware Road, walk to Marylebone, then an 8 minute Chiltern Railways train to Wembley Stadium station. Weekend flat fare on the Underground. Tap into the Underground with a US credit card or phone wallet, no advance purchase needed. The train ticket is best booked online in advance to skip the queue at Marylebone.

To Wembley, route 2: the Tube straight to Wembley WayIn quote

Included in your total

Underground from South Kensington to Wembley Park with one change at Green Park. This drops you onto Wembley Way, the walkway up to the stadium, with the pre game crowd. Nothing to book: tap in at South Kensington, change once, tap out at Wembley Park.

Good to know
